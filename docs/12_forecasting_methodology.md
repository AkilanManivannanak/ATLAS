# Forecasting methodology

* **Targets:** large-value **gross outflow** (the liquidity a bank must find; headline) and **net outflow** (sent − received), per institution per 15-minute bucket. Both are exogenous to the settlement policy, so there is no policy leakage.
* **Panel:** fixed topology (`topology_seed`) with day-specific arrival seeds, so "the same bank on another day" is well defined.
* **Splits:** whole days → train (12) / validation (4) / test (4). An out-of-distribution test uses days generated with 50% more payments and fatter amount tails.
* **Models:** historical mean (same bank and bucket), seasonal naive (last training day), ridge regression (time-of-day harmonics, tier, 8 intraday lags, rolling mean, cumulative flow, 3 system-activity lags, horizon), **graph-conditioned ridge** (adds the recent activity of the 3 institutions most correlated with the target on training days, a dependency graph learned from data), histogram gradient boosting (scikit-learn; the LightGBM-style baseline), and a **neural window model** (scikit-learn MLP, 64-32, early stopping).
* **Uncertainty:** split conformal prediction on validation days, using signed residual quantiles for the quantile forecasts and the absolute-residual quantile for a 90% interval. **Adaptive:** causal online quantile tracking of the interval half-width (Angelopoulos, Candès & Tibshirani, 2023; cf. adaptive conformal inference, Gibbs & Candès, 2021). The width used for bucket *b* is updated only with outcomes of buckets ≤ *b − h*, the information available at the forecast origin.
* **Metrics:** MAE, RMSE, MASE (scaled by the in-sample day-to-day seasonal-naive MAE), mean pinball loss, CRPS ≈ 2 × mean pinball over the quantile grid, empirical interval coverage, mean width, calibration error.
* **Not implemented:** LSTM and Temporal Fusion Transformer (no deep-learning framework in the dependency set). The neural window MLP is the neural baseline; it is not presented as either.
* **Generator v2** adds AR(1) market and bank activity factors, so intraday lags carry real signal (tested: lag-1 autocorrelation of de-seasonalised system outflow > 0.2).
